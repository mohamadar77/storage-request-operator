# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and professional.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements with evidence and clarity.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other project content that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through their GitHub profile. Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive reports. For security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
